# Privacy Policy for WindowIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
WindowIyagi is a tool that lists and controls open windows on Linux.

## Data Collection
WindowIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. Window information is read locally and never leaves your PC. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
WindowIyagi does not need an internet connection and does not send any data to external servers.
